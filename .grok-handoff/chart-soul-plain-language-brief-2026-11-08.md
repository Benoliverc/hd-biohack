## Recommendation

**Meaning-first, term-second.** Lead Chart and Soul with everyday human meaning; keep official Human Design names as secondary labels or inside Learn more / Why. Do not shrink Deep Dive essays. Do not add scores or soulmate language. Do not change mechanics.

Same pattern as today’s shipped Today Move (human sentence first, HD in Why).

## Why this fits Baseline

- Practical daily biohack, not HD textbook, not mystic oracle.
- Chart = natal blueprint (Snapshot short; Deep Dive teaches).
- Soul = aura between You (Me) and a Person; Love guidance only for Partner.
- Progressive disclosure: beginner-readable titles/teasers; depth one tap away.

## Research (tone)

Competitor matrix: Co-Star (blunt/thin), The Pattern (hide jargon — too far for Chart literacy), MyBodyGraph (newcomer tiers), Genetic Matrix/Jovian (tooling/jargon risk), Stella (everyday relationship language + tap-to-explain — steal outcomes, not AI/scores/Valentine).

Plain language: ODPHP, NHS (plain term first then jargon), NIH, NN/G progressive disclosure.

HD term tiers: keep Type/Strategy/Authority/Profile/Centers/Channels/Gates/Definition (explain); collapse hanging gate / EM roles / circuitry / Split bridges into Why; avoid PHS/Variables/Incarnation Cross on first layer.
